How this works

Claimable uses published 2026 federal rules (poverty guidelines, SNAP FY2027 tables, IRS 2026 tax credit limits) to estimate what you may qualify for. It isn't an application, and only the agency that runs each program can decide your eligibility. Dollar amounts are estimates. States often have extra programs and different limits, so call 211 for local help.
